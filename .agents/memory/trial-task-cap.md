---
name: Trial task cap
description: Why a global six-task ceiling is needed in addition to the trial's daily and time limits.
---

The free trial should visibly remain active until it reaches six rewarded trial tasks or its two-day expiry, whichever occurs first. Its status remains visible afterward as expired. VIP eligibility is independent and must not be stopped by the trial limit.

**Why:** A 48-hour window can overlap three calendar dates. Three tasks per calendar day alone could allow up to nine tasks before the two-day timer expires, contrary to the intended six-task trial.

**How to apply:** When changing task eligibility, task completion, or trial status displays, enforce and derive the total across the trial window on the server as well as the per-day cap, and keep every client status consistent with that same rule.
