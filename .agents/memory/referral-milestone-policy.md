---
name: Referral milestone policy
description: Product decisions behind direct-invite VIP milestone eligibility and promotion behavior.
---

Treat a directly invited member's VIP activation as a cumulative qualification. A paid activation or an administrative VIP grant counts once, even if that VIP access expires or is later downgraded; registration alone and second- or third-level referrals do not count.

**Why:** The user chose *direct invitees who activated VIP*, not all sign-ups, and requested once-only payouts at cumulative thresholds. Decreasing the count after expiry or downgrade would make previously granted milestones appear unearned.

**How to apply:** Keep qualification history independent of current subscription status and enforce one award per account and threshold when changing any VIP acquisition path. At the 250-member milestone, advance exactly one available VIP level: retain an active membership's expiry, or use the next package's configured term when inactive. Do not mark an unavailable next tier as awarded.
